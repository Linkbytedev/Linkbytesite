Who we are

About Linkbyte

An open-source studio building technology communities can trust.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create secure, privacy-focused technology that empowers communities and supports organizations. We want to help people stay in control of their digital lives — because we believe technology should be ethical, accessible, and built to strengthen trust, not exploit personal data.

Through open-source development and community-driven solutions, we strive to make digital experiences safer, more transparent, and more inclusive for everyone. Our tools are designed with integrity, security, and long-term trust in mind.

Our Vision

We envision a future where technology is open, secure, and respects every individual's privacy. A future where communities, organizations, and users can connect and grow without sacrificing trust or personal freedom.

Core Values

Privacy First

Every user deserves control over their personal data and digital identity.

Community Driven

We build technology that benefits people, supports organizations, and strengthens communities.

Transparency & Trust

We are committed to honesty, openness, and ethical development practices at every step.

Accessibility

Technology should be simple, inclusive, and available to everyone regardless of technical skill.

Innovation with Purpose

We create modern solutions that solve real problems while protecting the people who use them.
